Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version: Cadence runs entirely on your Mac. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your calendar data or anything else about you.

Who we are

Cadence is a macOS menu-bar app developed by Patrick McKinley ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

Information Cadence uses

To do its job, Cadence reads the following on your device. None of it is sent to us.

WhatWhyWhere it's kept

Presentation and call detection look at window positions and whether the microphone is in use. Cadence never reads your screen contents and never records or listens to audio, and it does not need Screen Recording or Microphone permission.

Services Cadence connects to

Cadence connects only to services you choose to use, directly from your Mac:

  • Google Calendar API — if you sign in with Google. Cadence requests read-only calendar access (calendar.readonly) using your own OAuth client. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • ICS / webcal feed hosts — whatever calendar subscription URLs you add.
  • Apple Maps — when estimating travel time, Cadence sends the meeting location and your approximate position to Apple's MapKit service. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Meeting services — when you click Join, the meeting link opens in your browser or the relevant app (Zoom, Teams, etc.).

Calendars you've added in macOS Calendar are read locally through Apple's EventKit framework; Cadence doesn't contact those providers itself.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.
  • No accounts and no Cadence servers.
  • We don't sell, share or receive any of your data.

Your choices

  • Calendar and location access can be granted or revoked any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Disconnect Google or remove ICS feeds in Cadence → Settings → Accounts. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the app and its preferences removes everything Cadence stores. Its Keychain items can be removed in Keychain Access (search for com.cadence.calendarmonitor).

Children

Cadence isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes
