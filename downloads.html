Installing

  1. Download the .dmg above.
  2. Open it and drag Cadence into Applications.
  3. Open Cadence from Applications. It lives in the menu bar, not the Dock.
  4. Allow calendar access when macOS asks, then add Google, Microsoft 365 or ICS calendars in Settings → Accounts.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later on an Apple silicon Mac. To update, download the newest version and drag it over the old one; your settings stay put. To check a download, compare it against the release's SHA256SUMS.txt: shasum -a 256 Cadence-*.dmg. Prefer to build it yourself? The source is on GitHub.
